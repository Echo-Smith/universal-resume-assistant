# Operational Work to Product Evidence

Use this reference when a candidate wants to translate operations, public-sector coordination, content work, brand communication, domain practice, or another non-software role into product-relevant evidence.

## Preserve the role boundary

- Keep the formal title visible or pair it with a functional scope after a separator.
- Do not rename operations work as product management merely because product concepts can describe it.
- Separate `transferable product evidence` from `formal product ownership`.

## Model the operating system

Extract evidence through:

`objective → users or stakeholders → supply → workflow → allocation or distribution → feedback → governance → outcome`

For an operating or service system, inspect:

- the user and operating objective;
- the service, content, or support supply;
- task taxonomy and prioritization;
- segment- or channel-specific delivery;
- contributor, reviewer, and stakeholder coordination;
- cadence, monitoring and feedback;
- quality, compliance and sensitive-data boundaries;
- the highest attributable outcome.

## Write without title laundering

Prefer:

- “围绕客户使用目标，组织知识更新、任务触达、问题回收与处理闭环”
- “根据用户分层和使用阶段调整服务节奏”
- “作为协同中间层推动跨部门问题完成定位、交接与验证”

Avoid:

- “负责客户成功产品路线图” when no such ownership existed;
- “通过产品策略提升续约” when only触达或问题处理 was measured;
- relabeling uploads, registrations or task counts as growth experiments.

## Attribution test

For each bullet, mark:

- personal action;
- product-like decision;
- team output;
- measured result;
- missing causal evidence.

Use numbers to establish scale or outcome, not to decorate every line. When a person coordinated work created by others, use “衔接、组织、推动、跟进” and state the team nature of the result.

## Business-side productization path

For operations, content, brand, or other non-software roles that have not formally owned a product, translate work through:

`repeated labor or workflow blockage → automation, data objects, or acceptance mechanism → reusable process → observed result`

Narrative template:

> In [formal role], targeting [repeated labor or workflow blockage], built [automation / data objects / acceptance mechanism] so that [concrete workflow change]; the same capability extends to [personal AI project], where [user problem] became [product flow + evaluation + iteration decision].

Rules:

- Keep the formal title visible; this path proves transferable product thinking, not a product title.
- Every mechanism must answer which observed failure it removed.
- Attribute team results to the team; use 衔接、组织、推动、独立构建 for personal actions.
- A personal AI project can close the loop (user → problem → product → real use → iteration) that the day job could not.
- Do not relabel operating work as product management; call it business-side productization.
- Keep future goals (for example, building a general agent tool) as direction only, never as completed experience.
